Meet Lihle

Hi, I am Lihle Lebese I am a proud mother of two beautiful girls and a loving wife My passion for baking and decorating started at home,where i enjoyed creating cakes for family and friends

What began as small hobby quickly grew into a business In 2015, I officially started Lihle Cakes & Kids Deco to share my creativity with the Mabopane and Pretoria North community. Since then I have been dedicated to making every celebration special with delicious cakes and uniqur decor

My goal is to bring joy,beauty, and unforgettable memories to every celebration Whether it's birthday, wedding, or corporate function, I believe every occasion deserves something sweet and stylish
